# car.glb

A 3D model of a white BMW 4 Series Gran Coupe (F36), generated with Hyper3D
Rodin (Gen-2.5) from two photographs on Wikimedia Commons, then decimated and
compressed for the Raspberry Pi in Blender.

Because it is derived from them, `car.glb` is shared under the same licence as
the source photos: **CC BY-SA 3.0 DE**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/de/deed.en).
